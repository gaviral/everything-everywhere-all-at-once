# Voice commands

The getting-back, finding, view, filing and fixing commands, with their variants. All are recognized by rule, with no model call, and only as the whole turn, except that “where was I?”, “next one” and “last one” also work at a turn's start or end, “stop listening” at its end, and a correction no rule reads (below). Recap, “make an app that …” and the yes or no to “Did you mean …?” are in the README; “what can I say?” lists every command. The README has the short list. Older words for some of these still work: see [Older words that still work](#older-words-that-still-work).

## The Unfinished thought card and moving around

Leave a topic whose last thought trailed off (“I need to pay rent before…”) and it's marked dropped, for you only. After 2.5 s without new words, the card offers the one you dropped last with your last words and Finish it and Later. The first Later hides it until at least 5 minutes have passed and you pause; it then returns once. A second Later hides it until that topic changes. “Where was I?”, “next one” and “last one” work as a whole turn or at its start or end; words before the command are placed first, and the command waits for them. “Go back to the drawing board” stays a thought. “Where was I?” or “go back” returns to where your last line trailed off (even on the topic you're on), else your latest dropped topic, else back from a detour: one line away from the topic you were on goes back to it, and a topic you reached without speaking (“next one”) goes back to where you last spoke; otherwise you're where you were, and it shows your newest point on the topic you're on (two lines there is staying, and asked again once back, it doesn't send you away again), or says “Nothing on the map yet: say a thought first”. “Next one” moves to the next topic in creation order, then wraps to the first, and “last one” (or “previous one”, “go back one”) moves the other way. “Where did I talk about …?”, “take me back to …” or “go to the one about …” (Take me there) jumps to the topic and highlights the point; if nothing matches, it says so (“I couldn't find “the lease”. Try a few words you used.”) and files nothing, except the words you said after the question, and a “take me back to …” that may be a saying (“Take me back to Paris”). “Find …”, “take me to …” and “go to …” search only when they point at the map (“find where I said …”, “go to the one about …”); “Find a dentist near the office” is a thought. “New topic” (or “create a new one”) places nothing, and your next thought starts a topic of its own. “Pictures”, “Cards”, “focus” and “show all” (or “go to the pictures”, “let's focus”) switch your own window's view, as the buttons do; “focus on this” (or “let's focus on that one”) focuses the card “this” means; with no topic to focus on, it says so. “What can I say?” (or “help”, “what can you do?”) opens Help, the list of these, on an empty map too; your next turn, Esc or a click outside closes it. “What just happened?” (or “what happened just now?”, “show me the notices”) opens the notices your window showed, newest first, in Notification history (with it off, the notice says where to switch it on); it lands nothing on the map, and in a sentence (“I don't know what just happened at work”) it's a thought. “Add milk to the shopping list” (or “put … in …”, “jot down … on …”, “add to the shopping list: …”) files the words at once in the topic whose title has those words, with what to add as the point and the whole turn kept as said; said as a kind of place no topic has yet (“the packing list”, “the Japan topic”) it starts that topic, and anything less sure (“Put the kids in the car”) is a thought the placer reads. “New topic about the garden shed” (or “New topic. …”, “Different topic, …”) starts a topic at once with every word after it, titled from them (“Garden shed”) until the AI finds a better title. “What was I thinking?” or “let me finish my thought” is “where was I?”, and so is saying you lost your place: “I lost my place”, “I lost my train of thought”, “I don't remember what I was saying”, “where did I leave off?” or “going back to what I was saying” (or “… you were saying”), only as the whole turn (“I don't remember” alone and “I lost my place in line” are thoughts); “Finish it” and “Later” are the Unfinished thought card's buttons while you have one, and “forget it” (or “never mind”) sets it aside as a second Later does, keeping its words on the map; each acts on the card you see. “Expand the garden” (or “show me everything under …”, “expand all the sub cards inside …”) opens a topic and every tangent under it until you fold it, “collapse this” folds yours, and “expand everything” or “collapse everything” does all of them; Option-click on Show or Hide does the same for one topic. “Add it to Reminders” adds the proposed action, as its button does, and “keep it” or “discard it” answers an app that's waiting. “Stop listening” (or “pause listening”, “mic off”) closes the mic: nothing after it is heard, sent or kept, and the header says Paused until you tap the mic. “Move that to the pottery topic” (or “put that in …”, “that goes in …”, “move that to a new topic”) moves what “that” means: the thought you just said while it's still finding its place, else the newest point on the card “this” means (the one you selected or point at, else the topic you're on); “my last thought” is always your latest. It goes with the words it carries on across a pause to the topic whose title has those words, the notice names it (“Moved “Book Lisbon flights” to Health”), and it takes you with it if it was your newest point on the topic you're on; a thought still finding its place moves once it lands, unless you say something else first. “Move it to Friday” stays a thought unless a title says Friday. “Rename this to Pottery class” (or “rename the garden topic to Backyard”, “call this topic …”) renames the topic “this” means, or the one named. “Delete this” (or “delete this thought”, “remove that point”) deletes the point you're on (focused or under the pointer; a point or card you focused stays “this” while you type in the input box, until Esc or a click elsewhere), else your latest thought; “forget the last one” (or “delete my last thought”) always takes your latest; “delete that” takes back what you just said, and with nothing just before it deletes as “delete this” does. On the map, a point's Move (shown on hover or focus) opens a list of topics to type into and pick from, or drag it onto a card; a click on a title edits it in place (on a touch screen a tap opens or folds the card, and a long press or a double tap edits the title); a point's Delete, or Delete or Backspace on a focused point, takes it off the map (a topic it leaves empty goes too), and focus moves to the next point. Each answers with Undo, and “scratch that” undoes it too, after a merge as well; “No, that goes in the pottery topic” moves the words just said. The move and the title are yours: the refiner never merges two topics you moved a thought between, an undone merge leaves a moved thought where you put it, it never renames a title you gave, and a pass that read a point you deleted lands nothing on that topic. All of these are recognized by rule, without a model call, and only the whole command counts: “go to the last one of the meetings”, “create a new one for the kitchen”, “put this in perspective”, “I bought a boat. What was I thinking?” and “stop listening to him” are thoughts. Every request takes the same words before it (“Okay,” “Well,” “Hey,” “Please,” “Can you …”), so “Can you recap?” and “Hey, where was I?” act; asked of someone partway through a turn (“I loved Japan. Would you go back?”), it stays a thought.

## Fixing a turn

“No, I meant …” (or “I was asking you to …”, “I meant …”) redoes your last turn, said in the last 2 minutes, as the request you name: a request is undone first (“Pictures”, then “No, I meant the cards”), and words that were kept as a thought (“Can we see all the little notes under the garden one”, then “No, I was asking you to open everything under it”) come off the map once the request acts, with Undo on the notice; the journal keeps them. A bare “No, …” corrects only a request: after a thought it's talk. When no rule reads the correction (“Expand the garden”, then “No, the bills”), one model call reads it against the app's list of actions and your topic titles, and answers with one action, which runs only if the same rules read it as that action; a correction of what you said (“No, I meant Tuesday”) stays a thought, and so does any correction without a key. “Scratch that” (or “undo that”) takes your last turn back: a filing or a move is undone, a view or a fold goes back to what the window showed before it (Cards said on Cards stays Cards), and a thought comes off the map with Undo, its point off its card too; said again, it puts it back. A correction redone as a search takes your earlier words off only once the search jumps, and an answer from the model that arrives after you've said something newer is dropped. “This” (“expand this”, “collapse this”, “focus on this”, “add this to Reminders”) is the card you selected (keyboard focus you moved there, or words selected in it), else the card under the pointer or the one it left in the last 2 s, else the topic you're on.

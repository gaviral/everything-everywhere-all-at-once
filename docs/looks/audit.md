# Across-the-room audit

Reviewed at 1920 × 1080 in the sample map's classic shell, one `?bricks=` feature at a time. [Screenshots](audit-shots/) show the opened features; all content is synthetic. Close and reopen were exercised on the 16 large example panels.

Remaining issues, worst first:

- **Demo candidates:** both example cards say the live demonstration is unavailable; they are review examples rather than playable demonstrations.
- **Sketch exchange:** both sessions start off and the drawing areas are blank until the audience enables them and adds a mark.
- **Pen and touch:** the destination examples start with no marks, so the result is hard to grasp without drawing.
- **Workflow inspector:** the example opens with every step pending; its result requires running the example.
- **Research walkthrough:** the initial screen is mostly empty space and the research note needs another click.
- **Advisor, project memory, part lenses and take checklist:** secondary labels and details remain small for a viewer three metres away.
- **Dependency sequences and demo choices:** technical labels and dense lists need explanation before a stranger can understand them.
- **Machine readiness and reminder receipts:** the small example cards and their status details remain difficult to read across a room.
- **Reload recovery:** the draft and simulated rebuild controls look like a test harness rather than a finished demonstration.
- **Live activity, sketch labels, dictation breakdown and populated variants:** dense controls or long explanatory copy compete with the main idea.
- **Care prompts:** some fine-print and controls fall below the first screen after enlarging the settings; scroll to reach them.
- **All screenshots:** the sample shell displays a no-AI-key notice; live Meta responses were not exercised.
